TechTivAI - Phase 2 Development Guidelines
Project Vision
TechTivAI is not a traditional AI agency website.
It is an AI Transformation Platform that combines the capabilities of an AI Consultant, AI Solutions Architect, AI Sales Engineer, Business Intelligence Platform, Proposal Generator, CRM, and Client Onboarding System into a single intelligent platform.
The primary objective is to automate the complete client acquisition and solution design lifecycle with minimal human intervention.
Instead of filling out a traditional contact form, every visitor should experience an AI-powered consultation that understands their business, identifies growth opportunities, designs a personalized AI transformation strategy, and guides them toward becoming a client.
Core Mission
Build an intelligent platform capable of:
* Understanding any business
* Identifying operational inefficiencies
* Detecting automation opportunities
* Auditing digital assets
* Designing AI solutions
* Recommending technology stacks
* Estimating implementation cost
* Generating professional proposals
* Handling client onboarding automatically
Platform Objectives
The platform should function as a digital AI consultant capable of replacing many traditional agency processes.
Instead of manually performing consultations, audits, proposal writing, project planning, pricing, and onboarding, TechTivAI should automate these activities using AI.
Core Platform Capabilities
1. AI Business Discovery
The platform should interact naturally with visitors using conversational AI.
It must collect:
* Business Information
* Company Goals
* Current Technology Stack
* Marketing Channels
* Sales Process
* Existing Automations
* Team Size
* Revenue Range
* Pain Points
* Budget
* Timeline
* Competitors
2. AI Business Intelligence
The platform should automatically analyze:
* Website
* SEO
* GEO Optimization
* Technical Infrastructure
* Performance
* Social Media
* Competitors
* Marketing
* Sales
* Customer Journey
* Trust Signals
* Analytics
* CRM
* Automation Readiness
3. AI Opportunity Detection
Instead of providing raw audit reports, the system should identify:
* Revenue Loss
* Growth Opportunities
* Automation Potential
* Marketing Gaps
* Sales Inefficiencies
* Customer Experience Issues
* AI Adoption Opportunities
4. AI Solution Architect
Based on collected business intelligence, AI should automatically generate:
* AI Transformation Strategy
* Automation Blueprint
* System Architecture
* Recommended Tech Stack
* AI Models
* AI Agents
* Integrations
* CRM Recommendations
* Infrastructure Recommendations
5. AI Automation Consultant
The platform should recommend business automation for:
* Marketing
* Sales
* Customer Support
* HR
* Finance
* Recruitment
* Operations
* Internal Workflows
* Lead Generation
* Reporting


6. AI Proposal Engine
Generate professional business documents automatically:
* Business Audit
* Solution Blueprint
* Proposal
* Timeline
* Budget
* Pricing
* ROI Analysis
* Scope of Work
* NDA
* Service Agreement
* Invoice
7. AI Sales Consultant
The AI should explain the proposal using text or voice, answer client questions, handle objections, recommend implementation options, and guide the client toward the next step.
8. AI Client Onboarding
Once approved, the platform should automatically:
* Generate legal documents
* Collect payments
* Create CRM records
* Create project workspace
* Create client portal
* Send onboarding emails
* Schedule kickoff meetings
9. AI Project Management
Provide clients with:
* Dashboard
* Timeline
* Progress Tracking
* Tasks
* Deliverables
* Documents
* Communication
* Support
